**CCXT Terminal is now on iOS.** 📱

A few weeks ago we launched CCXT Terminal on the web, so you could spot a move and trade it in one place. But markets don't wait until you're at your desk. So today, the whole terminal comes with you, on iPhone and iPad.

It's free, it's fast, and if you already use the web version, your setup is waiting the second you sign in.

[Download on the App Store →](https://apps.apple.com/app/ccxt-terminal/id6810760684?utm_source=ccxt_blog&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ios_launch&utm_content=ios_article_top)

## 🔔 Alerts that come to you

Alerts already reach you at your desk. Now they reach you everywhere else too. Set a price alert, and when the level hits, a push lands on your phone's lock screen, whether you're at lunch or on the train. Every alert lives in one list, marked Active or Triggered, so you always know what's still on watch.

## 🔄 One setup, every screen

Watchlists, alerts and account data sync in real time between web and mobile. Star a pair on the train, and it's on your desktop when you sit down. Set an alert at your desk, and the push finds you wherever you are.

## 🏎️ Speed you can feel

On a phone, a second of lag is the difference between catching the move and watching it go. The DOM, charts and Trades Feed stream live, screens open instantly, and your order goes out the moment you tap. It doesn't feel like a mobile app pretending to be a terminal. It flies.

## 🧰 Everything you know from the web

Everything you rely on in the web terminal is now on your phone too:

- **⚡ Liquidity you can watch move.** For a scalper, a shift in the order book often is the trade. The Scalping Order Book and Depth of Market (DOM) update in step with the live market, right on your phone.
- **🎯 Smart Orders.** See the move, send the order. Execution is instant, from the same screen you're watching.
- **🔍 Order flow, not just price.** Dual Cluster Charts (Volume and Bid × Ask) show which side is moving the market, and the live Trades Feed lets you read the tape as transactions land.
- **📈 Charts from 1 month to 15 seconds.** High-resolution and sharp on a phone, all the way down to 15-second candles for catching micro-impulses.
- **🔎 Screener.** Filter by market type, exchange and quote currency, sort the list, and star a pair to add it to your watchlist.
- **💼 Every account, one view.** Positions, orders, trade history and balances across Binance, Bybit and Hyperliquid, without opening three exchange apps.

## What stays on desktop

Custom workspaces, where you build your own layout from any modules you like. That's a desktop job, so in the mobile app every tool gets its own clean, focused screen instead. Everything else comes with you.

New to CCXT Terminal? It runs on the open-source CCXT library, the engine behind 5M+ downloads a year and 43,000+ GitHub stars. [Here's the full tour of the web version](/blog/meet-ccxt-terminal).

## Your funds stay on your exchange

The app is built on the same open-source CCXT code that developers already trust, and it's just as open about how it handles your money. It works purely as an execution layer. You link your exchanges through API keys, your funds never leave them, and we never hold or manage your capital.

> **🔐 Security tip:** give your API keys trading permissions only and leave withdrawals switched off. That way, the final say over your funds stays with you, on the exchange.
