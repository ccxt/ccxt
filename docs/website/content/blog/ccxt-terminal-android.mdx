**It’s here. CCXT Terminal is now on Android 🤖**

If you’ve been waiting for this one, thank you for your patience. When we launched on iOS, we promised Android was next. Today we’re keeping that promise.

Now CCXT Terminal is available to everyone: on the [web](https://terminal.ccxt.com), on [iPhone and iPad](https://apps.apple.com/app/ccxt-terminal/id6810760684), and on [Android phones and tablets](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ccxt.android&utm_source=ccxt_blog&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=android_launch&utm_content=android_article_intro). Whatever device you trade from, your terminal is there.

Spot a level on your laptop, set an alert and close the lid. When the price gets there, you’ll get a notification on your phone and can act on it right from the DOM.

It’s free, and if you already use CCXT Terminal on the web or iOS, your watchlists and alerts are waiting the second you sign in.

[Get it on Google Play →](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ccxt.android&utm_source=ccxt_blog&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=android_launch&utm_content=android_article_top)

## Everything you need for active trading, now on Android

- ⚡ **Advanced trading:** Scalping Order Book and DOM with live liquidity, plus Smart Orders with instant execution
- 🔍 **Market intelligence:** real-time Trade Feed, Dual Cluster Charts (Volume and Bid × Ask) and a Screener with quote sorting
- 📈 **Precision charting:** high-resolution charts from 1 month down to 15 seconds
- 💼 **Performance tracking:** orders, positions and trade history across Binance, Bybit and Hyperliquid
- 🔔 **Quick alerts:** set a level on your favorite pairs and get a push the moment it’s hit
- 🔄 **Unified workflow:** watchlists and trading data sync in real time across Android, iOS and Web

Custom workspaces stay on the desktop, where there’s room to build your own layout. On your phone, each tool opens full screen.

## Your funds stay on your exchange

CCXT Terminal works purely as an execution layer. You link your exchanges through API keys, your funds never leave them, and we never hold or manage your capital.

> **🔐 Security tip:** give your API keys trading permissions only and leave withdrawals switched off.
