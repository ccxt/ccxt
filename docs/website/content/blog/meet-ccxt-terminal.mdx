## From analysis to execution, in one workspace

Active traders know the setup: a charting platform for analysis, an exchange interface for fast execution, and one of those for every exchange you trade on. CCXT Terminal brings it all together.

Analysis and execution live side by side, so you can go from spotting a move to acting on it in a single motion. And it all runs on the same engine developers already trust in production.

## What's inside

### ⚡ Trade the live book

For a scalper, a shift in the order book often *is* the trade. The terminal is built for speed from the moment you see a move to the moment you act on it:

- **Liquidity you can watch move.** The Scalping Order Book and Depth of Market (DOM) update in step with the live market.
- **Smart Orders, instant execution.** Set up complex trades and send them straight from the interface.

### 🔍 See the raw order flow

We kept the order flow raw, down to the micro-moves you trade on, so you see exactly what's happening in the market:

- **15-second timeframes.** Catch every micro-impulse as it forms.
- **Dual Cluster Charts** (Volume and Bid × Ask). See who is driving price and where big players are absorbing it.
- **Trade Feed and Screener.** Read the tape live, then scan the day's biggest movers with custom quote sorting.

### 🗂️ All your accounts, one view

CCXT Terminal gives you one clear picture of every account you trade. Open positions, working orders, and trade history all sit in a single view, so your total exposure is always right in front of you.

At launch, the terminal supports **Binance, Bybit, and Hyperliquid**, and more exchanges are on the way.

### 🧩 Workspaces that fit how you trade

Arrange your setups exactly the way you like:

- **Workspace tabs.** Keep custom layouts in browser-style tabs along the bottom bar and switch between assets or strategies in one click.
- **Layout templates.** Start from a pre-built layout or a blank canvas in the **Templates** modal.

### 🔔 Alerts in one click

Price alerts that keep you in the flow:

- **Click to set.** Click any price level on the chart and the alert is live.
- **Drag to adjust.** Move alert levels right on the chart, or manage them all in the **Alerts** module.

## Your keys, your exchange, your call

The terminal runs on the same open-source CCXT codebase that developers already rely on, and we built it to be just as transparent about your money.

CCXT Terminal is purely an execution layer. You connect your exchange accounts with API keys, and your funds stay on your exchange accounts. We never hold or manage your capital.

> **🔐 Security tip:** Create API keys with trading permissions only, and keep withdrawals disabled. That way, control over your funds stays with you at the exchange level.
