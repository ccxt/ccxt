Step 1 of 5

Before you start

You are about to connect an exchange account, so your AI assistant can show your balances, orders and positions. It takes about five minutes: you create an “API key” on your exchange’s website and paste it here.

Your key stays on this computer

It will be saved in this file, here on your computer:

  • ✓Only your own user account on this computer can read that file.
  • ✓The key is sent only to the exchange you choose.
  • ✓It is never shown to the AI assistant, and never sent to CCXT.
  • ✓This page runs on your computer too: its address starts with 127.0.0.1, which means “this computer”.

Have ready

  • Your login for the exchange’s website.
  • Your phone or authenticator app, if the exchange asks for a security code.

Step 2 of 5

Get your key

An API key is a separate password made for apps. You create it on your exchange’s website, and you decide what it is allowed to do.

This exchange keeps the two separate. Add each one as its own account.

On the exchange, create a new API key with only these permissions

Look for “API Management” or “API keys” in your account or profile menu.

  • ✓
    Reading: onLets the assistant see balances, orders and positions.
  • ✓
    Trading: only if you want the assistant to place ordersLeave it off for a view-only setup.
  • ✕
    Withdrawals: keep them DISABLEDThis setup never needs to move money out of your account.
  • ✓
    Restrict the key to your IP address, if the exchange offers itThen the key only works from your own connection.

The exchange shows the secret part of the key only once. Keep that page open until you have pasted it in the next step.

Step 3 of 5

Paste your key

Copy each value from the exchange and paste it here. They are hidden as you type.

Where was this key created?

A key works in one place only. Pick the one you created it in.
